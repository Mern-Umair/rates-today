# Privacy policy: Rates Today (Android app)

Last updated 20 October 2026

Rates Today shows the day's dollar, gold, silver, petrol, diesel, LPG and currency rates in Pakistan. This policy
explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: the app has nothing to collect
from you. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no forms.

## Information we do not ask for

The app does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, CNIC number, location, contacts or bank details.
The converter works inside the app; the amounts you type are not sent anywhere.

## What the app downloads and stores

- The app fetches one small public file of the day's rates from the internet each time it opens. That request carries
  the usual technical details any server receives, such as your IP address and the app version, which are used only to
  deliver the file.
- The last downloaded rates are kept on your phone so the app still shows figures when there is no signal. Nothing
  else is stored, and uninstalling the app removes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is paid for by advertisements served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may use your
device's advertising ID and technical details about the device, as described in Google's privacy policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its advertising policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's Google settings
(Settings → Google → Ads). Users in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalised
ads are shown.

We do not receive any personal information from AdMob; we only see aggregate reports such as the number of ads shown.

## Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

## Permissions

The only permission the app uses is internet access, to download the rates file and the advertisements.

## Links to other sites

Pages link to the State Bank of Pakistan, OGRA and other publishers so you can check a figure at its source. Those
sites have their own privacy policies.
