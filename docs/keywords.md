# Keyword research: how people search for rates (20 October 2026)

Source: Google autocomplete with the searcher's country set to Pakistan (and to the UAE, Saudi
Arabia, UK, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman for the currency pages), plus Google's Year in Search 2025 for
Pakistan, where "Gold Rates in Pakistan" was in the top-10 local-news searches. Autocomplete
shows the exact phrases people type, in rough order of popularity. Every phrase below is mapped
to the page and the element (title, H1, H2, FAQ) that targets it.

## What was NOT done, and why

- **Separate city pages** (gold-rate-lahore, petrol-price-karachi): the official rate is the same in every
  city, so separate pages would be near-duplicates. Google treats those as doorway pages. The city
  searches are handled by a city section and table on the one page, with an honest note.
- **"Forecast" pages**: nobody can publish tomorrow's rate; an FAQ answers the question honestly.
- **Bank-specific rate pages** (al rajhi, nbp, remitly): those rates belong to the banks and change by the
  minute; an FAQ tells the reader how to compare them with the official rate.
- **Urdu-script pages**: the site is English with Roman Urdu questions in the FAQ, which is how the
  searches are typed. An Urdu-script version can come later as a separate `/ur/` section.

## Play Store (ASO)

Searched phrases on the Play Store follow the same words: "dollar rate", "gold rate pakistan", "currency rate",
"petrol price". Competing apps are named "Pakistan Gold Rate", "PAK Gold Rate Live", "Gold Price Pakistan",
"Gold Silver Live Rate Pakistan"; none combines dollar, gold, petrol and the Gulf currencies, which is the
gap this app's title and short description fill. See `store/listing.md`.
